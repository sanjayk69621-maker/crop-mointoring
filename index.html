Normalized Difference Vegetation Index

NDVI quantifies vegetation by measuring the difference between near-infrared (which vegetation strongly reflects) and red light (which vegetation absorbs for photosynthesis). High values signify dense, healthy chlorophyll biomass.

NDVI = (B8_NIR - B4_Red) / (B8_NIR + B4_Red)
